01What you need to know

  1. Your normal sales are now as big as last year's promo. Ordinary October days sell RM336–364k/day of JT, the same as YES 25's promo rate (RM333k/day). Without any promo, these 11 days would bring in about RM4.3–4.8M JT. The promo only counts if it adds on top of that.
  2. RM10.1M needs about +RM4.2M of JT on top of normal sales. That is roughly what MYP 26 added, and MYP 26 is the best promo you've run outside MATTA. The RM11.5M first draft needed 17% more than that record, so it became the stretch target.
  3. YEP should fill January, March and May 2027 seats, not run as a generic sale. Jan–Apr seats are projected to be ~68% full. January is at 53%, March at 48% and May at 61%. Discounts, creative and TC incentives go to those departures. April is already on track.
  4. Your bottleneck is how many deals the team can close, not leads or seats. The commit means about 55 closings a day, 7% above MYP 26's record. You need 16–20 people selling JT plus the online booking bot ("Robot1"). YES 25 ran with only 11 JT sellers and fell 25%.
  5. PT is shrinking: −46% revenue in October, and 2027 PT bookings are down 30%. The PT target only holds the trend. Fixing PT is a product job.
  6. WIGS 2027 GT (44,414 pax) is not reachable as written. It needs 2.39x growth vs 2026, and your history is 1.54–1.61x a year. The realistic commit is about 36k, with 44.4k as stretch. Plan airline deposits to 36k.

05What the TC data shows

Findings

  • Sales are concentrated: since 7/9, two CRM logins account for 60% of rows: "Ikhwan" (36.7%) and Robot1 (24.5%). The Ikhwan login logged almost nothing from May 2024 to Dec 2025, then 150–280 rows a month in 2026. Check whether it is one person or a shared entry login.
  • Too few sellers at YES 25: it ran with 11 JT TCs, down from 15 at YES 24, while normal daily sales had grown 46%. That explains much of the −25%.
  • The funnel leaks at the ask: only 30.5% of leads get a price and deadline (Sheet7 sample).
  • UH depends on one person: Zaid closed 39 of 59 UH closings.
  • The MFKL codes are fair booth logins: permanent TCs logged only 1–4 rows under their own names during MF 9/26.

What we can't see yet

  • Response time, follow-ups, ask-for-sale per TC: these need the SleekFlow DB (tables messages, conversation_daily, closes). It was blocked here.
  • Which WhatsApp lead became which sale: SleekFlow lead IDs match 0 of 23,844 CRM sale IDs. Fix by writing the SleekFlow ID into the CRM booking at closing.
  • Recent lead data: the SleekFlow lead sheet stopped syncing on 13/7/2026.
  • Lead stages: every lead stays "1. New", so stage data is useless.

08How this was checked

Four analysts (baseline, WIGS bridge, TC execution, promo levers) fed one plan. Three independent reviewers then recomputed about 120 numbers from the raw files and challenged realism and feasibility. The main changes they forced:

  • Commit cut from RM11.5M to RM10.1M and fixed on CRM closed_time. On CRM data, the draft's JT number needed 17% more than the record promo.
  • PT cut from RM1.0M to RM0.65M. The draft assumed ARPP 2,700; 2026 promos ran 1,818–2,460.
  • Leads needed cut from 9,420 to 3,900–5,100; surge staff from 25–30 to 6–8. Capacity is the real constraint.
  • Jan–Apr seat fill corrected from 48–58% to ≈68%, by projecting month by month.
  • Simpler automation. The AI chat responder and PT quote bot became a rules-based SleekFlow flow and pre-priced templates, because there is no live price and availability feed and only 4 weeks to build.
